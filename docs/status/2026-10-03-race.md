## Audit findings repaired

The previous gate checker awarded progress by proximity, even to a stationary
kart. Ranking incremented its index only when positions changed and produced
duplicate ranks. The old integration test directly edited lap/progress fields,
so a green result did not demonstrate a complete lap. Uniform route sampling
extrapolated beyond segment endpoints. Road triangles faced down, causing
karts to fall through and wedge against horizontal triangle edges.

Implemented: finite-width, finite-height forward swept gates in strict order;
start-line arming without free laps; teleport rejection; sub-tick finish times;
stable ranking; last-validated-gate recovery; automatic fall/off-route recovery;
idempotent results with a bounded DNF grace period; finished karts no longer
block the finish line. The HUD preserves director ordering and labels DNFs
without inventing times. Road winding and surface normals are corrected,
widths accompany arc-length samples, and AI uses a port-side short-horizon
controller that stays on the generated road in the tested race.
