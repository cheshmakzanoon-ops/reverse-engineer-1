## Implemented

The decoder distinguishes narrowly defined serialized-empty texture storage from
missing pixels in ordinary textures. Complete raw objects and typetrees remain
hashed and retained. Empty atlases do not receive fabricated PNGs.

Finalization and catalog verification require exact file-scoped native Font or
verified dynamic TMP font relationships. Names alone are insufficient. Malformed
storage, missing source-font data, unsupported owners, invalid byte arrays and
unresolved owner relationships remain failures. Source Font byte presence is
structural evidence, not proof that those bytes are a renderable font.

The catalog explicitly marks supported empty atlases non-renderable. Runtime
material conversion refuses them. Tests also reject forged ownership digests,
missing diagnostics and invented images even when their hashes are consistent.
The decoder recipe includes the new module; incompatible old checkpoints are
not silently reused. Same-recipe continuation remains tested.
